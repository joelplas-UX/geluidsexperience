Geluidsexperience is een professioneel geluid- en lichtinstallatiebedrijf met **25+ jaren ervaring**.

**Opdracht:** Maak een professioneel, premium redesign dat:
- Het expertise en 25+ jaren ervaring uitstraalt
- Portfolio-first benadering (foto's en video's centraal)
- Donkere, elegante aesthetic met neon/goud accenten
- Moderne, opvallende typografie
- Emotionele, overtuigende messaging
- Premium agency/professional services vibe

## 🎨 Design Direction Summary

### Color Palette
| Element | Color | Usage |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Background | #0f0f0f - #1a1a1a | Dark, premium base |
| Accent 1 | #00d4ff - #0088ff | Neon Blue (energy, tech) |
| Accent 2 | #d4af37 - #e8b923 | Gold (premium, warmth) |
| Text | #ffffff, #e8e8e8 | Contrast, readability |

### Typography Character
- **Headlines:** Bold, modern, confident (Montserrat, Inter, Playfair)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Roboto)
- **Tone:** Strong, professional, creative

### Inspiration
**Starshow (Den Haag)** - Premium agency aesthetic
- Donkere elegante achtergrond
- Portfolio-first approach
- Goud accenten
- Cinematic presentation
- Bold typography

---

## 🎬 Key Design Elements

### 1. Hero Section
- Cinematic full-width background (gradient, image, or subtle animation)
- Neon accents around logo/text
- Bold CTA with hover effect
- Overlay for text readability

### 2. Portfolio Section (PRIMARY FOCUS)
- **Cinematic carousel/slideshow:**
  - Full-width or large images
  - Smooth transitions
  - Neon accents on navigation/active states
  - Video play button overlays
- **Alternative:** Masonry grid with hover effects

### 3. About/Philosophy Section
- Split layout (text + visual)
- Highlight key quote with special styling
- Accent lines/dividers in gold
- Bold statement font for key phrases

### 4. Service Cards
- Professional custom icons (not emoji)
- Dark background with gold/neon hovers
- Glow effects on interaction
- Better spacing & typography

### 5. Contact Section
- Clean form with neon accents
- Prominent contact info
- Simple, elegant design
